The first release of agent-grange-viz as a Mac app.

- **The Stack** gives every teammate's grange a column and every layer between you and them a row: headscale, the tailnet path, gossip, the record your grange holds, their farm. A column is green down to the first layer that fails, and one word under it names the break.
- **The diagnosis** says each break in plain words, with the evidence and whose move it is. Hover any cell for the exact numbers, what its mark means and where the data comes from.
- **Two panels** sit above the grid: how fresh every record is against the 60 second expiry, and how the tailnet reaches each peer, directly or through the relay.
- **Your own side** is the first column. When nothing answers on the grange's port, the last picture freezes, dimmed, and the app says what Docker knows about the two containers.
- **It only reads**: the grange's local port and, if you allow it, the sidecar through Docker. It shows no agent's name or state and no pull request.
- **What today's grange cannot say yet**: each teammate's gossip cell, and rev and counter, wait for the grange to serve `GET /v1/mesh` (specified in `docs/openapi.yaml`). **Viewer ▸ Show the Demo** fills every row on a made-up team.
- **It needs macOS 27** or later on Apple silicon. On an earlier macOS the viewer still runs from source in a terminal; the README says how.
- **It does not update itself yet.** A new release is a new download from this page.
