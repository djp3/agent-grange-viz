A fix for a crash, and three things the window now tells you.

- **Fixed: the viewer quit when a teammate went quiet.** In 0.1.1 the app ended with a traceback about 45 seconds after any teammate's grange stopped sending heartbeats. That column now reads **silent**, and the diagnosis says how long ago its record was seen and when it expires.
- **A grange whose image is out of date is shown as not running.** It answers 503 to every request and gossips with nobody, so the app no longer keeps showing what it said before: the gossip, record and farm rows read *not known*, the top line and the diagnosis say the grange is not running, and the fix is given (`make update`). The headscale and tailnet rows stay live, since they come from your sidecar.
- **The title bar counts who you reach.** It reads 🐓 agent-grange-viz and the version, then one 📡 for each teammate's grange whose record yours holds, up to ten. The Dock, Mission Control and the window switcher show it too. The title now sits in the middle of the bar.
- **A crash leaves its details in the log.** If the viewer does stop, the traceback is in `~/Library/Logs/agent-grange-viz/agent-grange-viz.log`, where the window says to look.
- **Not known means not known.** Each teammate's gossip cell, and rev and counter, come from `GET /v1/mesh`. While your grange has not answered it those cells read *not known*, and their tips say that this is why. **Viewer ▸ Show the Demo** fills every row on a made-up team.
- **It needs macOS 27** or later on Apple silicon. On an earlier macOS the viewer still runs from source in a terminal; the README says how.
- **It does not update itself.** To get this version, download it from this page and replace the app you have.
