The farm news: what each grange's farm reported, as it reaches yours.

- **A new pane under the diagnosis.** Each line is one thing a farm reported: an agent started or ended, began waiting for its human, went busy or went idle, or a grange came onto the board or went off it with its agents. Newest first, with how long ago it reached your grange.
- **Pick a teammate's column (← →)** and the pane shows that grange's agents right now, by name, and then its own changes.
- **Yellow means it is waiting for you.** A row is yellow only for one of your own agents that is still waiting. Once you have answered it, the row goes plain.
- **It says "not known" when it does not know.** A grange that has just restarted, or that has not answered yet, is never shown as a farm with nobody in it.

The news starts when the app does. The grange keeps no history, so the pane holds only what changed while the app was open.

This is the one place the app shows agents by name. In a short window the rows above sit a little closer together to make room.
